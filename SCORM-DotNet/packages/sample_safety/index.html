Welcome to Workplace Safety

This course covers the essential principles of maintaining a safe working environment. You will learn about hazard identification, emergency procedures, and personal protective equipment.

Course Objectives

- Identify common workplace hazards
- Understand emergency evacuation procedures
- Know when and how to use PPE
- Pass the knowledge check with 67% or higher

⚠️

Hazard Identification

Hazards can be physical, chemical, biological, or ergonomic. Recognizing them is the first step to prevention.

Common Hazards

Physical: Wet floors, exposed wiring, falling objects
Chemical: Unlabeled containers, fume exposure
Ergonomic: Poor posture, repetitive movements
Biological: Mold, unsanitary conditions

Key Principle

Always report hazards immediately - even if they seem minor. Early reporting prevents accidents.

🚨

Emergency Procedures

Every employee must know the emergency evacuation routes and assembly points for their building.

Evacuation Steps

1. Stay calm and alert others nearby
2. Follow the marked evacuation route
3. Do NOT use elevators
4. Proceed to the designated assembly point
5. Report to your floor warden for headcount

Fire Extinguisher: P.A.S.S.

Pull the pin - Aim at the base - Squeeze the handle - Sweep side to side
